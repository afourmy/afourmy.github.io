Facts useful later (with book wording):
- ~20,000 protein-coding genes in human genome (fly ~14,500). Only ~1.5% of genomic DNA encodes proteins; ~2% more regulatory; 2017 estimate upper limit 25% functional. ~3.2 billion bp haploid genome.
- Diploid: two copies of every gene (except sex chromosomes). Alleles, homozygous/heterozygous, dominant/recessive.
  Recessive defective DNA-repair allele: two heterozygous parents -> 1/4 of children homozygous mutant, cancer-prone (Fig 1.2).
- Redundancy: "virtually all genes that operate to prevent runaway proliferation are present in two redundant copies, both of which must be inactivated" (sets up tumor suppressors, ch7). X chromosome ~900 genes, Y 78 genes -> 27 proteins; color blindness in males.
- Spontaneous point mutation rate in proliferating normal human cells: < 1 nucleotide per genome per cell division (~1 bp per 3 billion bp per division). Higher in cancer cells (ch12).
- ~3 x 10^13 cells in body; as many as 10^16 cells formed in a lifetime.
- Cancer cells: abnormal chromosomes noted as early as 1892. Aneuploidy (wrong chromosome number) in perhaps 90% of human solid tumors. Translocations, inversions, HSR (homogeneously staining region = tandem amplified copies, e.g. myc), double minutes (DMs, extrachromosomal amplified segments, e.g. HER2/neu), interstitial deletions (lose growth-inhibiting genes). Recurrent (non-random) alterations point to causal genes.
- Germ-line vs somatic mutation. Somatic mutation passed to all descendants of the cell: a clone.
- Genotype -> phenotype via proteins. Transcription, splicing (~93% of genes alternatively spliced; ~70,000 proteins from ~20,000 genes), PTMs (phosphorylation on Ser/Thr/Tyr; Ras has lipid tail).
- Cell types differ by which genes they express. ~10,000 proteins co-expressed in average differentiated cell. Housekeeping vs tissue-specific.
- Transcription factors: 1639 TF genes (2018), ~8% of protein-coding genes; bind 5-10 nt motifs in enhancers/promoters; combinatorial; one TF controls many genes (pleiotropy). Myc = anti-pausing factor for thousands of genes.
- Chromatin: nucleosome = histone octamer (H2A,H2B,H3,H4 x2), ~30 million nucleosomes per nucleus. Histone acetylation ~ active, methylation ~ repressed. H2AX phosphorylated at double-strand breaks.
- Epigenetic inheritance: phenotype passed through cell divisions without sequence change. CpG methylation near promoters represses; maintenance methyltransferase copies methylation onto hemi-methylated daughter DNA after replication (Fig 1.17) -> heritable silencing. Used to silence tumor suppressor genes (ch7).
- miRNAs ~22 nt, >500 species, regulate >60% of genes; let-7 represses ras; K-ras 3'UTR variant escaping let-7 -> up to 2x lung/ovarian cancer risk. Only ~20% of genes show close mRNA-protein correlation (76 lung cancers).
- Conservation: fly eyeless <-> mouse Pax-6 interchangeable; mouse biology ~ human biology. >500 protein kinases in one gene family.
- Gene cloning / reverse transcriptase / cDNA made cellular genes studiable in late 1970s.
