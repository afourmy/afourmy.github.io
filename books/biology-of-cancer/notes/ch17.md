# Ch 17 The Rational Treatment of Cancer (book p.781-~860; raw 101277-110485)

GIST: Most therapies still in use predate molecular oncology; surgery, radiation and cytotoxic chemo cure some cancers
(testicular 10% -> 90-95% with cisplatin; Hodgkin ABVD; childhood ALL ~90%; APL with ATRA + arsenic >90%), but rarely
advanced solid tumors. Two problems frame treatment: (1) diagnosis - screening finds many tumors that would never kill
(autopsy: >=2/3 of 80-year-old women carry breast carcinomas, ~4% die of it; melanoma incidence x6 while mortality flat);
expression signatures (70-gene breast) sort indolent from dangerous; (2) resistance - a 1 cm tumor has ~10^9 cells, resistant
mutants ~1 in 10^6, so a single drug meets ~1000 pre-existing resistant cells; three independent drugs -> 1 in 10^18 (why
combinations). Targeted drugs: only oncoproteins (hyperactive, druggable pockets, mostly kinases) are good targets; tumor
suppressors and caretakers can't be restored by drugs; oncogene addiction makes shutting the driver lethal. Gleevec (Bcr-Abl,
CML: <5% died in 5 years) is the model; relapse by kinase mutations (T315I) -> next-gen inhibitors; cancer stem cells survive
so treatment is lifelong. EGFR inhibitors work only in EGFR-mutant NSCLC (78% of responders mutant vs 6% of nonresponders),
relapse via T790M, MET amplification, SCLC transformation -> osimertinib etc.; venetoclax (Bcl-2, BH3 mimetic); Velcade
(proteasome, found by luck in myeloma); vemurafenib (B-Raf V600E melanoma; paradoxical activation in Ras-mutant cells).
Drug development: druggability, HTS, IC50 nM, selectivity, cell and animal models (poorly predictive), PK/PD, Phase I-III,
~1 in 100 succeed; synthetic lethality (PARP inhibitors in BRCA-deficient cells; checkpoint kinase inhibitors in p53-null).

Intro: US lung cancer 5-year survival 7% (1970) -> 19% (five decades later) (lead-time bias). Colon cancer mortality falls by screening.
  >1100 agents in development (2018). Prevention (HBV/HPV vaccines) will beat therapy.
17.1 Diagnosis: three classes: indolent (leave alone), aggressive (already disseminated), intermediate (treatable). Pancreatic NETs
  ("incidentalomas") ~3% of pancreatic tumors, 90% 5-year PFS vs pancreatic adenocarcinoma ~5% 5-year survival. Papillary thyroid in 1/3 at
  autopsy (Finland) vs 0.07% of US deaths. Cyclophosphamide (early 1980s dose) 5.7x AML. US 2018: ~266,000 invasive + 64,000 in situ breast
  cancers; ~41,000 deaths; only ~19% of invasive patients will ever develop life-threatening disease. Sidebar 17.1: at 80, >=2/3 of women carry
  breast carcinomas (~4% die of it); ~80% of men prostate carcinomas. Breast mortality flat most of 20th century while incidence rose.
  van de Vijver 2002: 70-gene signature (295 tumors; 151 patients 10-year follow-up), >90% accuracy; good-signature women gain ~nothing from
  adjuvant chemo. DLBCL: GCB vs ABC vs PMBL subtypes by expression (Rosenwald), different survival and drivers (ABC/PMBL NF-kB; GCB Bcl2,
  EZH2). scRNAseq, laser capture. Too many subtypes -> trials too small.
17.2 Pillars: surgery (early CRC resection 95% 5-year survival); radiation (Rontgen 1895; about half of patients get it; post-lumpectomy RT
  halves later mastectomy); chemo (mustard gas: Bari 1943, Yale 1942 lymphoma); aminopterin 1947 (first cured child); antimetabolites
  (methotrexate, 6-MP, fludarabine...); microtubule drugs (paclitaxel from Pacific yew; vinblastine/vincristine from Madagascar periwinkle,
  1958); cisplatin (1965; testicular cure 10% -> 90-95%).
17.3 Selectivity mostly unexplained (tumors have low proliferative index). Resistance: 1 cm tumor ~10^9 cells, resistance ~1 in 10^6 ->
  resistant variants inevitable; three-drug combination ~1 in 10^18. ABVD Hodgkin 5-year PFS 85-98%; childhood ALL ~90% cure. CHOP, FOLFOX,
  TIP. Sidebar 17.4: combinations may treat patient heterogeneity; childhood ALL survivors: 27.5% severe chronic condition at 25 years vs
  5.2% of siblings (identical twin photo). Table 17.5 resistance mechanisms (MDR pumps, detox, sanctuaries, target mutation, amplification,
  bypass, EMT, anti-apoptosis). PK (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion) vs PD.
17.4 Differentiation therapy: APL t(15;17) PML-RARa in >95%; ATRA -> neutrophils; ATRA + chemo 75-85% 5-year survival; ATRA + arsenic
  trioxide >90% cure. 13-cis-RA regresses oral premalignant lesions; RARb loss; antisense RARb mice lung carcinomas by 18 months.
  Mitotic catastrophe (G2/M checkpoint loss); p53-null cells depend on G2/M arrest -> ATM/MK2 inhibitors (synthetic lethality). PARP
  inhibitors vs BRCA1/2 (PARP trapping -> fork collapse -> DSB needs HR); resistance by BRCA reversion.
17.5 Targets: drugs inhibit, not restore -> gatekeepers and caretakers out; oncoproteins in. Oncogene addiction (Table 17.6: shutting off H-ras
  melanoma, K-ras lung, bcr-abl leukemia, myc various -> regression; neu, some myc/wnt relapse). HeLa: 50+ years later still need HPV18 E6/E7.
  Druggable = catalytic cleft; Ras GTPase can't be inhibited usefully; K-Ras G12C covalent inhibitors (~12% of Ras mutations in smokers' NSCLC).
  Transcription factors undruggable (except nuclear receptors: tamoxifen). Protein-protein interactions: nutlin (Mdm2-p53, 2003), tea EGCG
  (Bcl-xL Ki 490 nM, Bcl-2 335 nM), ICG-001 (b-catenin/CBP). 518 kinases, 90 tyrosine kinases; clefts similar.
17.6 Rational design successes: vemurafenib, venetoclax, ribociclib; mostly HTS. IC50 must go from 10-100 uM hits to nM. Gleevec: c-Abl IC50
  ~0.2 uM vs c-Fms ~6 uM; c-Src barely. Sacituzumab govitecan (Trop-2, SN-38 7.6 per antibody).
17.7 Cell models: DepMap: >27,000 genes x 1811 cell lines x 35 diseases (CRISPR). NCI-60. Shimada 2003: only 35 lines from 203 esophageal tumors
  (~17%) established, from the worst-prognosis patients. CCLE, GDSC, organoids. Hedgehog inhibitors: >=10 drugs, 86 trials, benefit only in BCC
  and medulloblastoma (pre-clinical doses too high). BaF3 IL-3/Bcr-Abl assay; T315I.
17.8 Animal models: xenografts poorly predictive; GEMM pancreatic tumors resistant to gemcitabine while transplanted versions respond (2009).
  PK AUC; Gleevec in mouse inhibits Kit to <1% within 1 h but rebounds by 8 h. Paclitaxel clearance varies 3x among 22 patients. Herceptin cardiac
  toxicity. ~11,000 genes expressed in a cancer cell mostly also in normal cells. ~80 My from rodents.
17.9 Phase I: dose escalation from ~1/10 animal toxic dose to maximum tolerated dose; PK; PD with surrogate (skin EGFR); therapeutic window.
17.10 Phase II/III; RECIST: CR (all <10 mm), PR (>=30% shrinkage), PD (>=20% growth), SD. Gemcitabine pancreatic: median 5.65 vs 4.41 months
  (5-FU) (Burris 1997) -> approved. Pancreatic 5-year survival <5%. ~1 in 100 drugs make it through. HER2 ~30% of breast cancers here
  (vs 15-20% in ch16!); B-Raf mutant ~60% of advanced melanomas.
17.11 Resistance: HER2/neu transgene off -> collapse, then relapse in most mice 1-9 months later. MDR1/P-glycoprotein (170 kD; 49 ABC transporters).
17.12 Bcl-2: BH3 mimetics; ABT-737 (NMR fragment linking), ABT-263 navitoclax (oral; hits Bcl-xL -> platelets die: thrombocytopenia),
  ABT-199 venetoclax (Bcl-2 selective) -> 17p-deleted CLL, AML in unfit patients (with other agents).
17.13 Gleevec history: Boveri 1914; Philadelphia chromosome 1960 (Nowell & Hungerford); t(9;22) 1973 (Rowley); ABL 1982; BCR; >95% of CML.
  Retrovirus Bcr-Abl -> mouse CML (1990). Imatinib: binds inactive conformation; inhibits Abl, Arg, PDGFRa/b, Kit (4 of ~90 TKs); CML cells inhibited
  at 40 nM; 1998 trial: all 31 patients remission; 6000 in trials by 2002; chronic phase: 90% hematologic response, 50% Ph undetectable
  (cytogenetic); blast crisis ~60% respond then relapse; 2006: <5% died of CML in 5 years; annual progression ~0.6% by year 5. qPCR: 29 vs 39
  cycles -> ~2^10 reduction; detects 1 CML cell in 10^5-10^6. Resistance: 29 of 32 relapses had kinase-domain mutations (13 residues), or
  amplification; T315I blocks imatinib, nilotinib, dasatinib; ponatinib (AP24534) works but vascular toxicity. AMN107 ~20x more potent.
  Hypereosinophilic syndrome with PDGFR fusion: complete response. GIST: Kit mutant ~85%, PDGFRA 3-5%; ~70% regress; ~50% worsen by 2 years
  (each regrowing nodule a new Kit mutation); sunitinib (2005). Stem cells not killed -> lifelong therapy; "dandelion effect". Ph+ B-ALL: dasatinib,
  ponatinib better. CML chronic phase like in situ carcinoma: single driver, few other mutations -> unusually good target.
17.14 EGFR: >=6 ligands; ~1/3 of carcinomas; iressa (gefitinib) and tarceva (erlotinib) ATP-site; iressa ~50x selective; EGFR vIII in ~40% of
  EGFR-overexpressing GBMs (half of GBMs overexpress). Half-lives: Tarceva ~36 h; cetuximab >5 d; Herceptin ~28 d. NSCLC: ~80% of lung cancer;
  <15% 5-year survival. Iressa 10% partial responses (West), 27% (Japan); women, nonsmokers, bronchioloalveolar. Tarceva +2 months after chemo
  failure. 2004 (Lynch, Paez): kinase-domain EGFR mutations: 78% of responders vs 6% of nonresponders; mutant EGFR in ~10% of Western vs ~30% of
  Asian NSCLC; ~100x more sensitive. BIM deletion polymorphism 12.3% of East Asians -> primary resistance to TKIs. KRAS, PI3K, PTEN predict
  non-response. Rash grade predicts survival: median 3.3 (no rash), 7.1 (grade 1), 11.1 months (grade >=2) (Tarceva). Relapse 6-18 months;
  T790M gatekeeper (raises ATP affinity) -> osimertinib (also first line); MET amplification; B-Raf; EMT; SCLC transformation (chemo).
17.15 Velcade (bortezomib): boronic acid; Ki 0.6 nM vs proteasome chymotryptic site; developed for cachexia; phase I myeloma patient: plasma cells
  41% -> 1% of marrow; myeloma: 55% objective responses + 25% halted; trial vs dexamethasone stopped 2003; progression 7 vs 3 months; sensitizes
  to melphalan. Mechanisms: IkB not degraded -> NF-kB blocked; misfolded antibody -> UPR -> apoptosis. Mantle cell lymphoma. Pay-for-response
  deals in Europe. Myeloma survival 3-5 years.
17.16 B-Raf: 2002 (Davies): mutant in >half of cutaneous melanomas; 90% V600E; ~10% of colon; mutually exclusive with Ras. Vemurafenib (2011):
  vs dacarbazine PFS +74%, mortality -20%, ~4 months more PFS; cutaneous SCCs arise (paradoxical activation of wild-type Raf dimers in Ras-active
  cells) -> sequence before use; combine with MEK inhibitor; drug holidays (resistant melanomas addicted to drug).
17.17 Synopsis: >200 diseases (8+ breast histotypes); US over-65 population x11 since 1900 vs x3 under 65; annual cancer deaths rising while
  age-adjusted falls. Melanoma incidence x6 in half a century, mortality flat; tracks screening rate. Breast cancer mortality -40% in some cohorts.
  Ovarian tumors: more subclonal diversification -> shorter PFS (9 tumors). Cancer gene census: 576 genes (>2% of genes): 533 somatic, 43 germ-line
  only. Kinome ~522: half have <=20 papers; 10 have >=5000; <10% targeted by FDA drugs. Chronic-disease model (like HIV).
