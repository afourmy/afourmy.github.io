# Ch 12 Shaping and Characterizing the Cancer Genome (book p.479-547; raw 63040-70781)

GIST: Mutations are the rate-limiting fuel of multi-step tumorigenesis, so the genome is guarded in layers:
(1) tissue architecture (rarely dividing, shielded stem cells; short-lived, shed differentiated cells),
(2) interception of mutagens (melanin, GSTs, ROS scavengers), (3) replication fidelity (polymerase 10^-5,
proofreading x10^-2, mismatch repair x10^-2 => ~10^-9 per bp per division), (4) repair (direct reversal MGMT,
BER, NER, HR, NHEJ), (5) mitotic checkpoints for chromosome number. Endogenous damage (deamination, depurination,
oxidation: up to 100,000 lesions per cell per day) dwarfs exogenous. Inherited caretaker defects (XP, HNPCC/Lynch,
BRCA1/2) accelerate tumorigenesis. Instability is a long-term investment (no immediate advantage). Tumors get
mutability either as MIN (mismatch repair loss: point mutations, indels) or CIN (chromosome number/structure
chaos), rarely both. Genome sequencing: tumor mutational burden spans >1000x; mutational signatures read the cause
(UV C>T at dipyrimidines, tobacco C>A, aging C>T at CpG, APOBEC). Driver vs passenger; mutual exclusivity within a
pathway. Repair defects are also weaknesses: MGMT-silenced gliomas respond to temozolomide; BRCA-mutant cells die from
PARP inhibitors (synthetic lethality).

Intro: adult ~4x10^13 cells; ~80 billion new cells per hour; ~2 m DNA per cell -> 160 billion m of DNA per hour (4000x around Earth).
12.1 Stem cells 0.1-1% of tissue cells (guesses). Colonic stem cell division -> up to 32 differentiated enterocytes. Colon epithelial
  cells live 5-7 days; keratinocytes 20-30 days; RBC ~120 days. Crypt: 4-6 slow stem cells at position 4-5, ~150 transit-amplifying cells
  dividing every ~12 h -> ~3500 enterocytes per villus; label cleared from crypt in 4 days. Mucus shields stem cells (Muc2-/- mice get
  adenomas/adenocarcinomas). Symmetric divisions refill lost stem cells; dedifferentiation.
12.2 Stem cells as cells of origin: 5-FU after initiation doesn't reduce skin tumors (target not cycling). CML Ph chromosome in B, T, myeloid,
  erythroid cells -> hematopoietic stem cell. ras in hair-follicle stem cells -> carcinomas; in differentiating keratinocytes -> regressing
  papillomas. AML lesions: proliferation class + differentiation-block class (erbB + erbA). Transit-amplifying cells may be the targets
  (more numerous, more divisions) and dedifferentiate.
12.3 Crypt stem cells apoptose after X-ray damage rather than repair. Stem cells pump dyes via Mdr1 (side population). 14+ DNA polymerases.
  Pol-delta proofreading-dead (D400A) mice: ~half homozygotes tumors by 1 year (49 homozygotes, 97 heterozygotes, 53 WT; hets fine).
  POLE/POLD1 proofreading mutations (germ line, somatic) -> ultramutated colon/endometrial (>100 mutations/Mb; up to 1 million substitutions).
  Error budget: polymerase ~1 in 10^5; proofreading misses ~1 in 10^2 -> 10^-7; MMR misses ~1 in 10^2 -> ~10^-9 per nucleotide ->
  "several miscopied bases per haploid genome per division". Microsatellite stutter (A7 -> T6/T8); MutS-alpha (MSH2/MSH6), MutL-alpha (MLH1/PMS2).
  MIN. ~10 dsDNA breaks per genome per S phase (collapsed forks). 518 kinase genes x 210 tumors: 1007 mutations; 1/3 of tumors none;
  MMR-deficient tumors 14-40 subs/Mb + 5-12 indels/Mb; temozolomide-treated gliomas high.
12.4 Endogenous damage: deamination C->U, A->hypoxanthine, G->xanthine; 5-methylC -> T (T:G escapes repair) -> CpG>TpG = the most
  frequent spontaneous mutation, accumulates linearly with age (intestinal stem cells, Blokzijl 2016: molecular clock).
  Depurination up to 10,000 purines lost per cell per day; depyrimidination 20-100x less (~500/day); 4000-50,000 abasic sites steady state.
  ROS: O2 -> superoxide -> H2O2 -> hydroxyl radical -> H2O; 1-2% of consumed O2 ends as ROS. 8-oxo-dG pairs with A -> G:C to T:A transversion
  (C>A). Inflammation oxidants (NO, superoxide, H2O2, hypochlorite) -> bystander mutation; smokers excrete ~50% more oxidized bases.
  Total: up to 100,000 bases altered per genome per day - exceeds exogenous damage in almost all tissues.
12.5 Exogenous: X-rays (80% energy strips electrons from water -> ROS -> DSBs). UV: pyrimidine dimers, >60% TT, ~30% CT, rest CC; 254 nm:
  71% CPD, 24% 6-4PP; CC>TT is the UV signature (TT repaired well); squamous cell skin carcinoma doubles per 10 degrees closer to equator;
  acral melanomas lack UV signature. Alkylating agents. P450s (57 genes) turn procarcinogens into ultimate carcinogens: BP -> BPDE
  (CYP1A1) -> guanine adducts in lung. Ethanol -> acetaldehyde adducts. Aflatoxin B1: G>T at p53 codon 249 (AGG->AGT) in ~half of HCCs in
  exposed areas; Qidong HCC 8x higher; HBV 7x, AFB1 3x, both 60x. Heterocyclic amines (PhIP 2/3 of dietary HCA) from cooked meat.
  Sidebar 12.4 Ames: 5000-10,000 natural chemicals/day; ~2000 mg burnt material, 1500 mg natural plant pesticides vs 0.1 mg synthetic
  pesticide residues; half of natural pesticides carcinogenic in rodents.
12.6 Shielding: melanin supranuclear caps (nuclei without caps 4x more UV damage); redheads ~4x melanoma risk; Australia highest skin
  cancer. SOD, catalase, vitamin C/E, bilirubin, urate. GSTs conjugate electrophiles; GSTP1 silenced by methylation in up to 90% of prostate carcinomas.
12.7 Repair: >160 proteins. MGMT direct reversal of O6-methylG (suicide enzyme, 1 alkyl per molecule); MGMT methylated in up to 40% of
  gliomas and CRCs, ~25% NSCLC, lymphoma, HNSCC. MGMT transgene protects mice from MNU lymphomas. Glioblastoma temozolomide: MGMT expressed
  12 months survival vs methylated 22 months. ENU in utero rats -> brain tumors (MGMT low in CNS; O6-alkylG 20x (ENU), 90x (MNU) more in brain than liver).
  BER (glycosylase -> APE -> pol-beta -> ligase; endogenous, non-distorting), NER (bulky, helix-distorting, cut 24 nt 5' and 5 nt 3' -> ~29-nt gap;
  UV, BPDE, AFB1); 80% of dimers repaired in 24 h; TCR vs GGR (GGR needs p53). Error-prone bypass polymerases (>=9); normal replicative error
  ~10^-5, bypass ~10^-2; pol-eta (XPV) puts AA opposite TT 95%; pol-kappa A opposite 8-oxo-dG; pol-beta overexpressed 10x in ovarian lines
  -> 3x mutation rate. APOBEC/AID cytidine deaminases (antibody hypermutation; APOBEC3B in breast, HN, lung).
12.8 XP (Hebra & Kaposi 1874): 2000x skin cancer before 20; ~100,000x tongue-tip SCC; median NMSC age 9 vs 67 years (Bradford 2011);
  8 complementation groups by cell fusion (XPA-XPG NER + XPV pol-eta); autosomal recessive; neurological 18%. Sunlight up to 100,000 lesions
  per skin cell per hour. Cockayne (TCR) no cancer, median death 12 years. HNPCC/Lynch 2-3% of colon cancer; 80% lifetime CRC risk;
  adenoma->carcinoma 2-3 years vs >10 years; MSH2+MLH1 85-90%; MSH6, PMS2 ~15%; LOH of remaining allele. TGF-bRII A10 tract: 9 of 11 MIN lines;
  100 of 111 MIN CRCs (A10 -> A9/A8 frameshift; 129 or 161 aa instead of 565). ~15% sporadic gastric/colorectal/endometrial MMR-deficient,
  almost all via MLH1 promoter methylation (field effect in adjacent endometrium). POLE/POLD tumors ultramutated, no MIN.
12.9 BRCA1/2: ~half of familial breast cancers; 70-80% familial ovarian; carriers 50-70% breast cancer by 70; ovarian 40-50% (BRCA1),
  10-20% (BRCA2) by 70; BRCA2 males >7x prostate before 65; pancreas. 20-30% sporadic ovarian lose BRCA1 by methylation. Caretakers not
  gatekeepers. HR uses sister chromatid (late S/G2); RAD51 filaments; BRCA2 has 8 BRC repeats; BRCA1 ~215 partners; gamma-H2AX. BRCA1 conditional
  loss -> mammary tumors after long latency; faster with p53 loss. NHEJ in G1 (error-prone, loses bases; also V(D)J; SCID). Nijmegen (NBS1).
  Ashkenazim: 3 founder alleles, ~2.5% carriers; 185delAG in 4.2% of Ashkenazi breast cancer patients; >80% risk by 80; risk by 50: 24% born
  before 1940 vs 67% after (nongenetic factors). >1500 BRCA1 alleles in general population. Table 12.1 syndromes (HNPCC, XP, AT, BRCA,
  Werner, Bloom, Fanconi, NBS, Li-Fraumeni, CHK2, RECQL4, MYH, PALB2).
12.10 Chromosome structure: >85% of solid tumors have chromosomal aberrations. Ig/TCR recombination misfires -> myc translocations.
  TMPRSS2-ERG ~50% of localized prostate cancer, with other ETS ~70%. EML4-ALK ~5% lung adenocarcinoma (inversion; mutually exclusive with
  EGFR). Chromothripsis (2011) ~25% of bone cancers; micronuclei; 77 rearrangements on 9p in a thyroid carcinoma.
12.11 Chromosome number: aneuploidy almost universal in solid tumors; CIN continues in culture (chr 8 copy number spread). MIN vs CIN
  roughly mutually exclusive in CRC (Lengauer 1998): either suffices -> mutability needed. Merotely main CIN source; normal cells ~1% of
  mitoses mis-segregate vs up to half in cancer cells; 14 SAC proteins; kinetochore binds 20-25 microtubules; extra centrosomes
  (multipolar spindles; Plk4 overexpression mice -> aneuploid tumors); CIN persists (unlike BFB window). ~100 yeast genes in mitosis.
12.12 Genome sequencing: HGP 1990-2003, ~$3 billion; <$1000 now (<$600 by 2021), beats Moore's law. TCGA, ICGC. ~600 genes recurrently mutated.
12.13 TMB: >7000 genomes; >1000-fold range (pilocytic astrocytoma <1/Mb to melanoma, lung). Smokers' lung adenocarcinoma ~175 nonsynonymous
  mutations vs ~10x fewer in never-smokers. POLE >200/Mb. Signatures: 6 substitution classes x 16 contexts = 96 channels; ~30 signatures
  (2013); Sig 1 CpG C>T (aging), Sig 2/13 APOBEC (TCW), Sig 4 tobacco C>A, Sig 7 UV. Aristolochic acid A>T (CAG/TAG context) in Romanian kidney
  cancers and Asian urothelial. MMR -> indels at homopolymers; BRCA -> indels/NHEJ scars. CNVs from read depth.
12.14 Driver vs passenger: recurrence above background (gene length!), mutation pattern (hotspot vs truncating), functional tests, CRISPR screens.
12.15 Inter-tumoral heterogeneity: lung adenocarcinoma ~40 significantly mutated genes; 499 LUAD: KRAS 32%, EGFR 13%, BRAF 6%,
  MET 2%, ERBB2 2%, RIT1 2%... mutually exclusive (mostly one RTK/Ras-pathway hit per tumor; rest have NF1-type TSG loss). Intra-tumoral:
  truncal vs branch vs private mutations; target truncal.
12.16 Synopsis: germ line balances evolvability vs stability; soma wants zero. p53 = permissive for survival of mutants. Replication stress
  (oncogene-driven origin firing) -> focal amplifications/deletions, early (dysplasia). Instability double-edged: too much = death.
  PARP1 inhibitor: SSBs -> DSBs at forks -> need HR -> BRCA2-/- ES cells killed at ~1000x lower drug concentration (Farmer 2005).
  Platinum works better in BRCA carriers. Yeast 5600 genes: 153 replication/repair, 88 repair only; human repair genes 130 (2001) -> 176 (2012).
  H2AX ~15% of H2A; gamma-H2AX spans up to 2 Mb around DSB; H2AX at 11q23. ~10 progeroid syndromes from repair defects.
