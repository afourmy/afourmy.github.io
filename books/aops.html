Exercise. Pipe A can fill a pool in 5 hours, while pipe B can fill it in 4 hours. How long will it take to fill the pool if both are operating at the same time?

Solution.

Pipe A fills the pool in 5 hours, so its rate is $\frac{1}{5}$ of the pool per hour.

Pipe B fills the pool in 4 hours, so its rate is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the pool per hour.

When both pipes operate simultaneously, their combined rate is: $$\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{4}{20} + \frac{5}{20} = \frac{9}{20} \text{ of the pool per hour}$$

To find the time needed to fill one complete pool: $$t = \frac{1}{\frac{9}{20}} = \frac{20}{9} \text{ hours} = 2\frac{2}{9} \text{ hours} \approx 2 \text{ hours and } 13 \text{ minutes}$$
