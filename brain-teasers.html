Exercise. Two dice are rolled. You are told that at least one of them shows a 6. What is the probability that the other die also shows a 6?

Solution.

There are 36 equally likely outcomes when rolling two dice. The event "at least one die shows a 6" contains 11 outcomes: $(1,6),(2,6),(3,6),(4,6),(5,6),(6,6),(6,1),(6,2),(6,3),(6,4),(6,5)$. Of these, only $(6,6)$ has both dice showing a 6. By conditional probability:

$$P(\text{both are 6} \mid \text{at least one is 6}) = \frac{1}{11}.$$

Exercise. A train departs from $A$ toward $B$ at $300$ km/h. Simultaneously, a fly departs from $B$ toward $A$ at $10$ km/h, with $AB = 1000$ km. Each time the train and the fly meet, the train immediately reverses direction; the fly never reverses. When the fly arrives at $A$, what total distance has the train traveled?

Solution.

The fly travels $1000$ km at $10$ km/h, so it arrives after $t = 100$ hours. Throughout this entire duration, the train is in motion at $300$ km/h regardless of direction. Therefore the total distance traveled by the train is:

$$300 \times 100 = 30{,}000 \text{ km}.$$

Exercise. You have $1$ dollar and earn interest of $x\%$ per year, compounded annually. How much do you have after $y$ years?

Solution.

After one year, the balance is multiplied by $1 + \frac{x}{100}$. Applying this factor $y$ times:

$$\left(1 + \frac{x}{100}\right)^{y} \text{ dollars}.$$
