Sample Mathematics

The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus links differentiation and integration. If $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and $F$ is an antiderivative of $f$, then:

$$\int_a^b f(x)\, dx = F(b) - F(a)$$

Euler's identity unites five fundamental constants:

$$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$

The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality in an inner product space:

$$\left|\langle u, v \rangle\right|^2 \leq \langle u, u \rangle \cdot \langle v, v \rangle$$
